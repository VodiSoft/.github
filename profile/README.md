## 🏢 About VodiSoft

**[VodiSoft](https://vodisoft.com)** is a software partner delivering corporate web, mobile, and desktop applications, custom business software, and system integrations — from analysis all the way to launch and ongoing support. With **19+ completed projects**, **5+ years of experience**, and clients across **6 sectors** (manufacturing, retail, finance, professional services, logistics, and education), VodiSoft combines fixed-scope engagements with a transparent, four-step delivery process: discovery, planning, development, and launch & support.

Beyond client work, VodiSoft is also an active contributor to the open-source .NET ecosystem — **FmgLib** is VodiSoft's own suite of free, high-quality .NET libraries, with **78,500+ downloads** across **12 packages**, used by developers worldwide.
